// 00 — Profile

From operational chaos to AI products that ship — and that people actually use.

For 3.5+ years I've found the real bottleneck behind a request, designed the solution, and built the working thing — mostly solo, across e-commerce and digital publishing.

I treat AI as a co-creation partner: context engineering, prompt architecture, and relentless iteration until the output is trustworthy enough to hand to a real user. My background in buyer psychology and operations is why I care about adoption as much as the build.

I'm honest about the seams — I'm not a traditional engineer. I'm the person who owns the problem, the spec, the prototype, and the rollout, and who knows when to pull in an engineer for the hard parts.
